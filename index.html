01. Starbucks Rewards Analytics

Python · Pandas · Power BI · DAX · RFM Analysis

Developed a customer analytics project using Starbucks Rewards data, analyzing over 138,000 transactions and 76,000 promotional offer records to investigate purchasing behavior, promotional effectiveness, customer segmentation, and revenue concentration.

Built five interactive Power BI dashboards and applied Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) analysis to identify customer segments and evaluate customer value.

Key findings: $1.78M in revenue, a 54.38% promotional offer completion rate, and 53.38% of revenue generated by the top 20% of customers.

05. Longitudinal & Mixed-Effects Modeling

R · Linear Mixed Models · Longitudinal Analysis · Statistical Modeling
